Billing & Debt Recovery

Debt negotiation moved from phone calls to self-service, with BRL 4.5M recovered in the first year.

The case in five words

Outcome

The Problem

Debt recovery lived on the phone.

Debt negotiations were mostly handled by phone, without standardized processes. This affected both participants and the teams handling recovery:

  • Participants had no unified digital flow to negotiate independently or follow their progress.
  • Teams handled negotiations manually, keeping the process dependent on phone contact.
  • BRL 300M+ in potential recovery was at stake in a process that depended on manual negotiation.
My Role

Discovery to delivery, end to end.

As Senior Product Designer, I worked on the conception and definition of the digital experience, collaborating with commercial strategy, IT, and participant relationship teams. I mapped user flows, created wireframes, designed high-fidelity prototypes, defined requirements, created dashboards, and validated with stakeholders and the legal team.

The Decisions

Designing access and self-service.

01

One unified flow for all profiles

Rather than separate interfaces for participants, employees, and partners, we built a single flow with role-based access. The shared structure let us maintain improvements in one place.

One shared flow to maintain across all profiles.

  1. 01 Access

    Enter the negotiation journey.

  2. 02 Contract identification

    Identify the participant using contract details.

  3. 03 View outstanding debts

    Review the outstanding debt before starting a negotiation.

  4. 04 Start negotiation

    Begin the self-service negotiation.

  5. 05 Choose payment method

    Review the available payment options.

  6. 06 Confirm negotiation

    Review and validate the proposed agreement.

  7. 07 Pay invoice(s)

    Proceed with payment of the agreement's payment documents.

  8. 08 Debts cleared

    The journey reaches debt clearance after the outstanding amounts are paid.

02

Verified access without account creation

Participants with cancelled plans couldn't create accounts. We used contract data for identification and email or SMS verification so they could access the negotiation flow without creating an account.

Access for participants who couldn't register through the usual process.

03

Self-service negotiation as the default

The design target was to let participants complete negotiations in under five minutes without a phone call. Flow length, payment options, and confirmation steps were evaluated against that target. We monitored completion time in Clarity for a period.

Participants could negotiate independently, without calling the team.
