I led product and design for a new app serving members of CASSI's partner health plans.

Company

CASSI

Role

Product & Design Lead

Delivery

iOS & Android

One network. A missing digital experience.

Members of partner health plans could use CASSI's provider network but had no dedicated app. Basic information and services were fragmented. The challenge was to give this audience a clear way to manage their care.

From discovery to delivery.

I led product and design from concept through launch and post-launch support. I helped define requirements with stakeholders, guided the visual identity, and co-created the style guide and component library.

Designed around everyday needs.

Keep the essentials close.

The home screen brings card information and key services together. Members can view, copy and share their digital ID card.

Find care in the provider network.

Provider-network search is available in the app. Results appear on a map, with a list view as an alternative. This screen shows dermatology results and the option to return to the list.

Make room for more than one account.

Multiple accounts can stay signed in on one device. Members can switch between them without logging out.

Build a foundation for the next release.

The app predates Harmony and has its own component library and visual language. This Figma excerpt documents icon-button states and touch areas.

A dedicated app. Its own foundation.

CASSI launched a dedicated app for partner-plan members on iOS and Android, with a documented component library for the product.
