## One network. A missing digital experience.

Members of partner health plans could use CASSI's provider network but had no dedicated app. Basic information and services were fragmented. The challenge was to give this audience a clear way to manage their care.

## From discovery to delivery.

I led product and design from concept through launch and post-launch support. I helped define requirements with stakeholders, guided the visual identity, and co-created the style guide and component library.

## Designed around everyday needs.

### Keep the essentials close.

The home screen brings card information and key services together. Members can view, copy and share their digital ID card, with biometric login for access.

### Make room for more than one account.

Account switching supports family members and different partner contracts on one device. Notifications, authorization details and monthly usage statements bring more information into the app.

### Build a foundation for the next release.

We documented reusable components in Figma using Atomic Design principles, with accessibility guiding the design. A dedicated visual identity connected the new product to CASSI's ecosystem.

## A new app. A shared foundation.

CASSI launched a dedicated product for partner-plan members. The component library reduced rework and established a foundation for future services.
