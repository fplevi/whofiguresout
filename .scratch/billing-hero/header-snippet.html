Billing & Debt Recovery

Debt negotiation moved from phone calls to self-service, with BRL 4.5M recovered in the first year.

The case in five words

  • Problem _ _ _ _ _Phone Debt negotiation lived mostly on phone calls, with no standard process.
  • Decision _ _ _ _ _ _ _One flow One flow for participants, employees, and partners, with role-based access.
  • Decision _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _No account Contract data plus an email or SMS code let cancelled-plan participants in without creating an account.
  • Trade-off _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _Reactivation Plan reactivation was offered up front, but participants could always negotiate without it.
  • Result _ _ _ _ _ _ _BRL 4.5M Recovered in the first year, as negotiation moved to self-service.
